# VideoStreamX - User Guide

## Introduction
VideoStreamX is a simple video browsing and streaming application for iOS that provides an intuitive interface to explore and watch video content. This guide will help you understand how to navigate and use the application effectively.

## App Navigation

### Home Screen
When you open VideoStreamX, you'll land on the Home screen where you can:
- View featured or recommended videos
- Browse through the main content offerings
- Access primary video navigation options

### Content Library
Access additional content by tapping the "Others" tab where you can:
- Browse videos organized in a collection view layout
- Explore different categories of content
- Discover additional video offerings

## How to Use

### Navigating Between Sections
1. Use the tab bar at the bottom of the screen to switch between Home and Others sections
2. Tap the "Home" tab (house icon) to return to the main screen
3. Tap the "Others" tab (grid icon) to browse the collection view

### Browsing Content
- Scroll through available content on each screen
- Tap on any video thumbnail to view more details
- Navigate through hierarchical content using the navigation bar

## Video Player Controls

Once you've selected a video to watch, you can control playback with these features:

### Basic Controls
- **Play/Pause**: Tap the center of the video player or the play/pause button to toggle playback
- **Resume**: After pausing, tap the play button to continue watching from where you left off

### Navigation Controls
- **Forward**: Tap the forward button to jump ahead (typically 10 seconds)
- **Backward**: Tap the backward button to jump back (typically 10 seconds)
- **Seek**: Drag the progress bar to any point in the video to jump directly to that timestamp

### Playback Options
- **Playback Rate**: Adjust video speed (0.5x, 0.75x, 1.0x, 1.25x, 1.5x) by tapping the speed control button
- **Video Quality**: Select your preferred video quality based on your network connection
- **Fullscreen Toggle**: Rotate your device or tap the expand button to enter/exit fullscreen mode

### Player Settings
- **Auto-Play**: Toggle automatic playback of videos when opened or when the next video in a playlist is reached
  - Access this setting by tapping the settings gear icon in the player
  - When enabled, videos will automatically start playing
  - When disabled, you'll need to manually press play for each video
- **Auto-Hide Controls**: Choose whether player controls automatically hide during playback
  - Access this setting through the player settings menu
  - When enabled, controls will disappear after a few seconds of inactivity
  - When disabled, controls will remain visible until manually dismissed
  - Tap anywhere on the screen to show controls again when they're hidden

## App Structure

### Two Main Sections
1. **Home** - Primary interface for accessing content
   - Identified by the house icon in the tab bar
   - Provides main navigation and featured content

2. **Others** - Collection-based content browser
   - Identified by the grid icon in the tab bar
   - Displays content in a grid layout for easy browsing
